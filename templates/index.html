Cloud storage notice

Render Free has an ephemeral filesystem. Saved flights, missions, and uploaded metadata can disappear on restart or redeploy. Export your logbook CSV regularly. Permanent storage requires an external database or another host with included persistent storage.

OpenSky and VATSIM are public services subject to availability and rate limits. OpenAI requires a server-side API key; no key is ever entered in the browser.

Mission briefs are simulations, not dispatch releases or verified regulatory, weather, fuel, or airport information.
